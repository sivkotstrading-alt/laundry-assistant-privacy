Privacy Policy – Laundry Assistant

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Developer: Sivkots Software

Summary

Laundry Assistant does not collect personal data itself. Your last selection (colour and fabric), your Pro status and your acceptance of the user agreement are stored only on your device. The app works offline.

Camera (Pro label scanner)

The label scanner uses the camera to read care labels. Images are processed on your device only and are never stored or uploaded. You can revoke camera access at any time in your device settings.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the Advertising ID and device information to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy and the AdMob partner page. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through a consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. Pro users see no ads.

Purchases

The Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Your purchase is tied to your Google account.

Affiliate links

Some buttons link to Amazon. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases; the price you pay is the same. Amazon's own privacy policy applies on their site.

Analytics and tracking

No analytics or other third-party trackers are included.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
